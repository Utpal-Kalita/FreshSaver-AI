# Problem Statement Sources

These sources support the global food-waste problem statement in the FreshSaver
presentation. Keep the scopes distinct: total global food waste is not equivalent
to waste caused by expiry or date labels.

## UNEP Food Waste Index Report 2024

Source:

- https://www.unep.org/resources/publication/food-waste-index-report-2024
- https://www.unep.org/news-and-stories/press-release/world-squanders-over-1-billion-meals-day-un-report

Reported for 2022:

- 1.05 billion tonnes of food waste were generated, including inedible parts.
- This represented 132 kg per capita and almost one-fifth of food available to consumers.
- Households accounted for 60%, food service 28%, and retail 12%.
- Food loss and waste generate an estimated 8–10% of annual global greenhouse-gas emissions.
- The global economic toll of food loss and waste is estimated at roughly US$1 trillion.

Approved deck wording:

> In 2022, 1.05 billion tonnes of food waste were generated across retail, food
> service and households. Retail represented 12% of that total.

Do not say that all 1.05 billion tonnes were caused by expiry dates.

## European Commission Date-Marking Study

Source:

- https://food.ec.europa.eu/food-safety/food-waste/eu-actions-against-food-waste/date-marking-and-food-waste-prevention_en

The European Commission cites a 2018 market study estimating that up to 10% of food
waste generated annually in the EU is linked to date marking.

Approved deck wording:

> A European Commission study estimates that up to 10% of annual EU food waste is
> linked to date marking.

Do not generalize this EU estimate to the entire world, and do not equate a date
mark with a food-safety determination.

## UN Food Loss And Waste Context

Source:

- https://www.un.org/en/observances/end-food-waste-day

The UN summarizes that approximately 13.2% of food produced is lost between harvest
and retail, while approximately 19% of total global food production is wasted across
households, food service and retail. FreshSaver focuses on the retail decision point,
not upstream agricultural loss.
